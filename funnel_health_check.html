How this calculator works

What this tool does

This calculator turns your real ad account numbers into a clear picture of your funnel's economics — and shows what happens to your profit if you improve key steps of the funnel, or if you let them slide. No theory. Just your numbers, three scenarios, one page.

How to use it

  1. Pick your Traffic Source: Google Search (you pay per click, no real CPM) or Meta / Display (you pay per impression, CTR drives clicks). The calculator switches its logic automatically based on this choice.
  2. Fill in your current, real numbers from your ad account and store — for Google Search, your CPC; for Meta/Display, your CPM and CTR. Then Monthly Ad Budget, Website Conversion Rate, Average Order Value (AOV), Cost of Goods Sold, Fixed Monthly Costs, Payment/Transaction Fees, and Fulfilment Cost per Order.
  3. Optionally open Advanced Settings to change the assumptions behind the Optimized and Neglected scenarios (how much CTR, Conversion Rate and CPM shift in each one). Sensible defaults are pre-filled.
  4. Click Calculate my funnel. Everything else — Clicks, effective CPC, Estimated Purchases, CPA, Revenue, Costs, Contribution Profit, ROAS and Contribution ROI — is calculated automatically.
  5. The Optimized Scenario column shows the upside if the assumptions above are hit. The Neglected Scenario column shows what happens if those same metrics slide by the assumed amount instead.
  6. Compare the three Contribution Profit and Contribution ROI numbers side by side, and check the Break-even Analysis below the table to see how far your current numbers sit from break-even.

What each metric means

  • Traffic Source — Google Search (CPC-bid, no real CPM) or Meta/Display (CPM-bid, CTR drives clicks) — this switches which formulas the page uses.
  • CPM — Cost per 1,000 impressions — used only for Meta/Display.
  • CTR — Click-through rate — share of people who see the ad and click it. Used only for Meta/Display.
  • CPC — Cost per click. For Google Search, this is your input (you bid per click). For Meta/Display, it's calculated as Ad Budget divided by Clicks.
  • Conversion Rate — Share of website visitors who complete a purchase.
  • AOV — Average Order Value — average revenue per purchase.
  • Estimated Purchases — Clicks × Conversion Rate, kept unrounded so every downstream number stays accurate.
  • CPA — Cost per acquisition — Ad Budget divided by Estimated Purchases.
  • ROAS — Return on Ad Spend — Revenue divided by Ad Budget.
  • Contribution Profit — Revenue minus Ad Spend, COGS, and all modelled costs (fixed + variable). It reflects the profitability of the funnel itself, not your company's full net profit.
  • Contribution ROI — Contribution Profit divided by Total Modelled Costs. Compares estimated contribution profit with all costs included in this model.
  • Break-even Revenue/ROAS/Purchases — the revenue, ROAS and purchase volume needed just to cover Ad Spend and Fixed Costs, given your COGS % and transaction fee %.

A note on the numbers

CTR, CPM and Conversion Rate are modelled independently for scenario analysis — in real campaigns, changes in one metric may affect the others. This model uses simplified, linear assumptions to make the mechanics visible. It's a directional diagnostic, not a media plan — real campaigns involve seasonality, audience saturation and creative fatigue that a static model can't capture. Treat the gap between scenarios as a signal of where to focus, not a guaranteed forecast.
